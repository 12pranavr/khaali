How one Tatkal booking travels through the machine
10:00 · the window opens
15 minutes. Requests only collect — nothing is processed on arrival, so there is no race to win.
→
You book & block the fare
UPI · scan the QR on your phone · card · netbanking · wallet PIN. Your bank blocks ₹175 and debits nothing — the way an IPO application works. The block is what makes your entry real.
→
10:15 · the window closes
A booking at second 1 and a booking at minute 14 sit in the bowl as perfect equals.
→
Allotment runs once
Seeded and published — anyone can replay the draw and get the same 40 names. No discretion, no hidden priority.
✓ Allotted
Your berth is named — S4/60, Lower — and only now is the ₹175 taken: the block becomes the ticket. One tap prints the PNR. The bell rings to tell you.
✗ Not allotted
The block is released and ₹0 was debited — no refund, because nothing was taken. The bell rings to say so. Then the khaali part: the card shows the berths that need no window at all — book normally, or take the guaranteed seat-hop.
Meanwhile, in the bot lane
3 farms fire ~500 requests
In 40 milliseconds. Under the starting gun this won everything. Here, speed buys nothing.
→
Identity filter
500 requests trace back to 3 verified persons. Verification happened weeks ago, calmly — no OTP panic at 9:59.
→
Cap & fare
4 entries per person per month, each with money locked — 12 paid entries stand among ~150. The farm floats ₹2,100 a round to win a handful of berths. The business dies of arithmetic, not policing.
Why speed stops mattering
The agent shop’s only weapon was being first, and inside the bowl there is no first. Its {{ ftBots }} bot accounts collapse to a handful of chits because each verified person gets four a month — flooding the bowl needs warehouses of real, capped humans, and the business dies economically instead of being policed. Finance proved this cure on its own bot war: batch the window, clear it as one auction, break ties by lottery. Stock exchanges’ version had to discover a price each batch; Tatkal’s price is already printed on the fare table, so the bowl only has to decide who — a solved problem. The allocation you see here runs on the same engine that locks khaali’s berths: all-or-nothing, deterministic, auditable.
Simulated population, real mechanism. The 80% bot-traffic figure and 3.03 crore deactivated IDs are from Railways’ own statements; the batch-auction cure is the accepted market-design answer to speed races (Budish, Cramton & Shim 2015; IEX’s speed bump). This page is a policy demo — it books nothing.
